# W Click GUI

Adds a **W** button to the main menu and the pause menu. It opens a mobile-style click GUI.

## Features
- **CBFH**: auto frame-perfect click assist with a 1-20 frame window. An early click near an orb is held and fired on the exact tick you touch it.
- **FPS Display**
- **Practice Helper**: turns practice mode on when a level starts.
- **Click Counter**
